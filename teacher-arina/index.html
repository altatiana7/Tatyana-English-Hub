Reference Materials

Учительская библиотека: материалы для всех недель можно готовить заранее.

Week 1

Present Simple

Habits, routines, facts. Markers: usually, often, every day, sometimes, never.

Form: I/you/we/they work • he/she/it works.

Week 1

Present Continuous

Actions happening now or around now. Markers: now, at the moment, currently, look!, listen!

Form: am/is/are + V-ing.

Week 1

Present Perfect

Result/experience connected with the present. Markers: already, just, yet, ever, never.

Form: have/has + V3.

Week 1

Present Perfect Continuous

Duration or recent activity with a visible result. Markers: for, since, all day, lately.

Form: have/has been + V-ing.

Week 1

Irregular Verbs

Remember three forms: V1 • V2 • V3. V2 is used in Past Simple; V3 is used in Perfect forms and Passive Voice.

Week 1 • Test 1

Present Tenses + Irregular Verbs

12 заданий. После проверки появится балл и короткий код для преподавателя. Правильные ответы здесь не показываются.

1. Arina usually ___ her homework after dinner.
2. Look! It ___ heavily now.
3. I ___ this book already, so you can borrow it.
4. She ___ English for three years, and she still studies every week.
5. We ___ to school by bus every day.
6. Why are your hands dirty? — I ___ my bike.
7. He ___ never ___ sushi before.
8. Listen! Someone ___ at the door.
9. Choose the correct three forms of GO.
10. Choose the correct three forms of WRITE.
11. Choose the correct three forms of TAKE.
12. Choose the correct three forms of SEE.

Warm-up discussion

“Family is not an important thing, it’s everything.” — Michael J. Fox

Do you agree? Use: unconditional support, core human connection, maintain family bonds.

Who am I? What is it?

Choose the correct expression for each definition.

1. This person earns money for the family.

2. To look exactly like someone else.

3. A wife and a husband.

4. A child with no brothers or sisters.

5. To feel sad and depressed.

6. A perfect example for others.

7. To be very similar in appearance.

8. To resemble an older family member.

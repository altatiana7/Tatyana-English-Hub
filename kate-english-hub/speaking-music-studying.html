Fact 1
Pleasant music can sometimes improve your mood while studying.
TRUE. A better mood may help motivation, although it does not guarantee better concentration.
Fact 2
Songs with lyrics are usually the best choice for difficult reading.
FALSE. Lyrics can compete with language processing and make comprehension harder.
Fact 3
Fast and loud music may reduce performance on demanding tasks.
TRUE. High speed plus high volume can be especially distracting.
Fact 4
The “Mozart effect” proves that Mozart makes people permanently smarter.
FALSE. The popular claim is exaggerated; mood and arousal are more plausible explanations.
Fact 5
Instrumental music is often a safer option than word-heavy songs.
TRUE. Quiet instrumental music is less likely to compete with reading or vocabulary work.
Fact 6
The best study soundtrack can depend on both the person and the task.
TRUE. Routine tasks and deep-reading tasks may require different conditions, and people react differently to noise.

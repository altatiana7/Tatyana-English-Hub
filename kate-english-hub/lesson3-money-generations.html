Will Gen Z make cash cool again?

Prediction before evidence. Decide what sounds plausible and explain why.

CASE STUDY
GEN Z + MONEY

Could physical cash become useful again?

Consider control, debt, budgeting, inflation, side hustles and social-media money trends. The point is not to prove that “Gen Z uses cash”, but to examine why some young adults might deliberately choose it in certain situations.

Cash can make overspending feel more visible.
Young adults who grew up digital may still use cash for budgeting.
Using cash means a person rejects technology.
Concern about debt may influence payment choices.

CRITICAL THINKING

Gen Z is a cash-only generation.

Some people may use cash because it makes a spending limit more visible.

Older generations are always better at saving.

Cost of living can affect how any generation spends and saves.

Social-media trends can influence financial habits, but they do not describe every person.

If someone uses cash, they must be wary of all digital technology.

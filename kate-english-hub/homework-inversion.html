How inversion works

Inversion changes the usual word order to emphasise a point.

Never + auxiliary + subject + main verb
Never have I seen anything like this!
Да я в жизни ничего подобного не видела!

Use do / does / did when there is no auxiliary. After did, use the base form: did she realise, not did she realised.

Remember

After Only after or Not until, inversion happens in the main clause: Only after I got home did I notice the problem.

Hardly ... when and No sooner ... than usually use Past Perfect for the first event and Past Simple for the second.

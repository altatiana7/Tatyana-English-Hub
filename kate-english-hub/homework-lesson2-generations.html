Generations Timeline

Read the short guide, then check what you remember.

The dates below are commonly used ranges. Generation labels are broad generalisations, so individuals can be very different.

Baby Boomers

1946–1964
  • stability
  • hard work
  • traditional careers

Generation X

1965–1980
  • independence
  • work-life balance
  • adaptability

Millennials / Gen Y

1981–1996
  • technology
  • flexibility
  • experiences

Generation Z

1997–2012
  • social media
  • digital life
  • diversity

Generation Alpha

2013 onwards
  • AI
  • smart technology
  • online learning

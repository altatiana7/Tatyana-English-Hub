Similarities

  • Both generations tend to …
  • They have a lot in common when it comes to …
  • One thing they share is …
  • Neither generation is particularly …

Differences

  • Unlike older generations, younger people tend to …
  • Whereas Baby Boomers often …, Gen Z tends to …
  • Compared with previous generations, …
  • Younger generations are more likely to …, while older generations may …

Talking about trends

  • tend to + verb: Millennials tend to value experiences.
  • be more / less likely to + verb
  • increasingly: People are increasingly working remotely.
  • no longer: Many people no longer expect one job for life.
  • these days / compared with

Generation gap

  • see eye to eye = agree
  • be stuck in your ways = be unwilling to change habits or opinions
  • keep up with the times = change as society changes
  • have something in common = share an interest, opinion or characteristic

Speaking Lab

Use careful language: compare people without turning generalisations into facts.

Avoid stereotypes

Don't say:
“Gen Z are lazy.”

“Older people don't understand technology.”
Say:
“Some people argue that Gen Z has a different attitude towards work.”

“Some older adults may feel less confident using new technology.”

Natural speaking phrases

  • From my experience, …
  • Personally, I don't think age matters that much.
  • I can see both sides.
  • That depends on the individual.
  • There may be some truth in that.
  • I'm not sure I'd generalise like that.

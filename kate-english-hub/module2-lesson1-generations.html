Warm-up

7–10 minutes · speaking

Question 1

Look at the screen, Kate. There are 20 modules. If you look at Module 2, it’s about generations. Tell me, what comes to your mind when you hear the word generation?

Question 2

Do you think young people and older people always understand each other? Why / Why not?

Teacher cue: use “Small Steps, Big Progress” as the lesson motto and encourage a full answer, not one-word replies.
2

Vocabulary

15 minutes · introduce and personalise
tech-savvy
технически подкованный

A person who is very good with computers and gadgets.

Ask Kate: Are you tech-savvy? Is your mom tech-savvy?
digital native
цифровой гражданин / человек цифрового поколения

A person who grew up surrounded by digital technology.

Say: People who were born with smartphones and the Internet around them are digital natives. That’s your generation.
traditionalist
традиционалист / консерватор

Someone who prefers traditional habits or ways of doing things.

Example: Someone who prefers physical books, newspapers and paper letters.
3

Guess the Generation

15 minutes · speaking game
Teacher: “Let’s play a game. I describe a generation, and you guess which one it is.”

Description 1

These people often value hierarchy, safety and job stability. They may prefer traditional books and face-to-face communication. Who are they?

Baby Boomers

Description 2

These people have never really known a world without the Internet. Smartphones are a natural part of their everyday life. Who are they?

Gen Z
Extension: ask Kate to create one description herself for Gen X, Millennials or Gen Alpha.
4

Case Study: Phone Call vs Message

10 minutes · discussion

Office situation

Imagine an office. A Baby Boomer boss wants to discuss everything by phone. A Gen Z employee dislikes unexpected phone calls and prefers writing messages in Telegram.

Who is right? What could they do to solve the problem?

Follow-up

Which communication style is more efficient in this situation, and why?

Follow-up

What compromise could make both people comfortable?

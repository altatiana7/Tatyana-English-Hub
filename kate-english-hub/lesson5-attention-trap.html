07 · Grammar Lab

Inversion & Emphasis

Rarely do people consider their digital footprint.

Not only can social media build a career, but it can also destroy one.

Only after the scandal went viral did she apologise.

Rewrite: People rarely think about the consequences of their online behaviour.

Passive Reporting

People believe that she deleted the post. → She is believed to have deleted the post.

Rewrite: People say the creator has lost several sponsors.

Conditionals recap: Had she known the consequences, would she have posted it?

Modal perfects

must have + V3logical deduction about the past
He must have underestimated the risks.
might / may / could have + V3possible explanation
She might have lacked practical experience.
can’t / couldn’t have + V3strong belief it did not happen
He can’t have considered every possible outcome.
should have + V3criticism or regret
Schools should have taught us basic financial skills.
needn’t have + V3it happened, but was unnecessary
We needn’t have memorised every detail.

Important: could have can also express a missed opportunity: Schools could have offered more practical projects.

Past modals: quick reference

must have + V3I'm almost sure it happened. She must have cheated.
Наверняка, должно быть.
can't have + V3I'm almost sure it didn't. He can't have written it himself.
Не может быть, чтобы.
might / may have + V3One possible explanation. They might have run out of money.
Возможно.
could have + V3It was possible, but it didn't happen. He could have gone to Oxford.
Мог бы (но не стал).
should(n't) have + V3Criticism or regret. They should have taught us this.
Следовало / не следовало.
needn't have + V3You did it, and it was unnecessary. I needn't have revised chapter 9.
Зря сделал.
didn't need to + VIt wasn't necessary, so you probably didn't do it. I didn't need to revise it.
Не было необходимости.

Trap: mustn't have is not used for deduction. Say can't have.

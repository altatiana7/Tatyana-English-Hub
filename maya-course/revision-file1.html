Maya · A trip to a hotel

Plan · 60 min · one storyline: back after a break → a hotel trip

  1. 1 Back (8 min). Quizlet: she says the word and a sentence about herself. Interview: question sounds only, 40 seconds, flip to check. Rapid Categories: 20 seconds per round.
  2. 2 Homework (10 min). 1D test as bombs, then Grammar Doctor. Bridge: Present Continuous = what is happening now → photos.
  3. 3 What’s happening? (8 min). Hotel photos: she describes in Present Continuous and names the problem; flip and listen.
  4. 4 Rob calls (10 min). Watch once: how many calls? Call 1 / Call 2 cards, then Audio Gap Race (real voices). Notice: problem vs solution, I’ll + verb.
  5. 5 Your call (8 min). Call Simulator (choose, say aloud), Politeness Lab.
  6. 6 Rooms (8 min). Mystery Box: she is the guest, you are Reception; then Unexpected reply / Repair Clinic.
  7. 7 English File 5th. Not finished last lesson: Story so far and Six months later with their tasks. Bridge: Emma is now a hotel guest.
  8. 8 Talk Show (8 min). Gist: how many times does Emma call Reception? T/F, order, retell as Emma.
  9. 9 Final (5 min). Jeopardy: each finished column opens one digit of the lock.

Watch for

  1. Stative verbs in Continuous: I am needing, she is preferring.
  2. Present Simple for now in photo description: she stands instead of she is standing.
  3. doesn’t work instead of isn’t working; Could you to send…

Maya · Revision

Plan · 60 min · after a two-week break

  1. Warm-up (8 min). Quizlet: she says the word and a sentence about herself, then flip. 60-Second Challenge: she names words aloud, you type them. Odd One Out: why is it different? Speaking 1–4.
  2. Homework (12 min). 1D test as bombs: 15 seconds, 4 cards. Grammar Doctor: she finds and fixes the mistake, you flip.
  3. Games (8 min). Floor is Lava for tenses, Rapid Categories for words.
  4. Photo (8 min). Open the hotspots one by one, then 30-second talk.
  5. Rob calls (10 min). Watch once: how many times does Rob call Reception? Then Audio Gap Race.
  6. Reception (10 min). Say it politely, then Mystery Box: she is the guest, you are Reception. Swap.
  7. Jeopardy (5 min). Each finished column opens one digit of the lock.

Watch for

  1. Stative verbs in Continuous: I am needing, she is preferring.
  2. Present Simple for now in the photo: she sits instead of she is sitting.
  3. doesn't work instead of isn't working for broken things.

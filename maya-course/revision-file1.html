Maya · Revision

Plan · 60 min · after a two-week break

  1. Ten Faces. Button in Warm-up: File 1 questions. Button in Reception: guests call with a problem, Maya answers as Reception.
  2. Warm-up (8 min). Quizlet: she says the word and a sentence about herself, then flip. 60-Second Challenge: she names words aloud, you type them. Odd One Out: why is it different? Speaking 1–4.
  3. Homework (12 min). 1D test as bombs: 15 seconds, 4 cards. Grammar Doctor: she finds and fixes the mistake, you flip.
  4. Games (8 min). Floor is Lava for tenses, Rapid Categories for words.
  5. Photo (8 min). Open the hotspots one by one, then 30-second talk.
  6. English File 4th + 5th (12 min) – not finished last lesson. Three videos, tasks on the right; flip cards for Call 1/2 and the T/F. Bridge: “Rob was in room 613. Emma is in a different hotel and a different story, but what does a guest still need to say when something stops working?”
  7. Listening · Rob (10 min) – not finished last lesson. Watch once: how many times does Rob call Reception? Then Audio Gap Race.
  8. Reception (10 min). Who says it? (lines from the book dialogue), Say it politely, then Mystery Box: 12 photos of hotel problems; she opens a box, says the problem from the photo, presses Start and calls you; you answer as Reception; flip to compare. Swap roles.
  9. Phone call (10 min). Call Simulator: one Reception line at a time, she chooses, says it aloud, Next. Politeness Lab. Repair Clinic: she corrects aloud, you flip. Unexpected reply: you read Reception’s line, she reacts, flip for the model.
  10. Talk Show (8 min). Play: how many times does Emma call Reception? True/False, order the events, then Maya retells as Emma (40 s). Script only at the end.
  11. Jeopardy (5 min). Each finished column opens one digit of the lock.

Watch for

  1. Stative verbs in Continuous: I am needing, she is preferring.
  2. Present Simple for now in the photo: she sits instead of she is sitting.
  3. doesn't work instead of isn't working for broken things.

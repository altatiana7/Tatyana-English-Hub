Maya · 2A Lost… and found?

Plan · 60 min

  1. 1 Back (15 min). Hotel Quizlet: she says the phrase from the photo, then flip. Can Maya remember Emma's problems without seeing the vocabulary? Then the hotel phrases game and the two Past Simple starters.
  2. 2 Lost… (7 min). Emma at the airport: predict (passport / phone / tickets / money? What should Emma do?), then reveal. Holiday Quizlet: say the phrase, flip, make a Past Simple sentence.
  3. 3 Mountain (12 min). Teacher-made retelling of the textbook story. Read, then Hide text: she corrects the false statements from memory, then flip.
  4. 4 Marta (15 min). Track 2.1, original textbook audio. First listen: gist. Pause after “I couldn’t find my ID card” for a prediction. Pause after “my flight to Lyon left without me”: what should Marta do? Second listen: 8 questions.
  5. 5 Past Simple (11 min). Regular or irregular? Flip the verbs, then Rules. Error cards: she corrects orally, then flip. Final 90 seconds: at least three past verbs and one negative sentence.

Watch out!

could has no did: couldn’t find (not didn’t could)
be: I / he / she / it was, you / we / they were

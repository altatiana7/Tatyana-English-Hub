1 · Warm-up

Interview · answer in 40 seconds
Agree, disagree or not sure?
Teacher: you read the question aloud, Nikita answers; tap the circle for 40 seconds. The question text is on the back of the card only. The poll has no right answer: ask “Why do you think so?”. Aim: lead to charisma and persuasion, no names yet.

2 · Words and phrases

Cards 1–12: twelve personality adjectives, with the recording of the word and of the whole card.
Cards 13–18: six phrases about power and belief.
Say the word, explain it in your own words, then turn the card.
Teacher: Nikita says the word and gives the meaning in his own words first, then flips. “Listen” plays the natural recording of the word (front) or of the whole card (back). Cards 13–18 are the six phrases. If time is short, take the six hardest words.

3 · Matching

Match the word and the meaning
Teacher: three rounds of four pairs, a correct pair turns green. If Nikita mixes up expressions, go back to the cards on the left and ask for his own example.

4 · Sorting · positive, negative or depends?

One possible grouping, not the only key. Positive: persuasive, resilient, humble. Negative: manipulative, arrogant, domineering, ruthless. Depends: charismatic, self-assured, devoted, sceptical, ambitious. Ask: “When would it become a weakness?”. The main idea: the category depends on context. Accept reasoned alternatives.

5 · Floor is lava

A wrong button turns into lava and Nikita tries another one. If a sentence needs three tries, return to the card in block 2. Aim: 12 out of 12.

6 · Case file · from behaviour to trait

Build your argument

1. This behaviour could suggest that the person is…

2. The strongest evidence is…

3. However, another interpretation is…

A trait is an interpretation, not a fact. Several answers can be defensible.

Teacher: after each answer ask Nikita for the strongest evidence and for one other reading of the same behaviour. These are invented cases, not claims about real people.

7 · Word formation

Continuous text as on the FIPI site, the root word is on the right. Nikita says the form aloud, then taps the gap. Answers: charismatic, devotion, persuasive, Leadership, loyalty, reliability, arrogant, intolerant, destruction, ambitious. Notice the suffixes -ity, -ion, -ous, -ive, -ant. A noun can be a subject without an article, like Leadership here. The fact about the Expositor is historical background; the other judgements are opinions or invented examples.

8 · Motive detective · must, might, can’t have

must have + V3
strong inference from evidence
might / could have + V3
a possibility, the evidence is not enough
can’t have + V3
strong inference that it was impossible or very unlikely
Careful: do not use must have as proof of someone’s private motives.
must have = strong conclusion; can’t have = almost certainly impossible; might have = possibility. After each answer ask which piece of evidence decides it.

9 · Error hunter

One wrong word in each sentence: a word or a modal. Nikita taps it and sees the correction. Modal corrections: must have → might have; can’t have → might not have. Aim: he explains in his own words why the word is wrong.

10 · Jeopardy

Nikita picks a cell, you read the question; the second tap shows the answer, the third closes the cell. The order can change.

11 · Speed Game

Read the definition.
Tap the word within 15 seconds.
No hints: recall the meanings.
15 seconds per answer. If time runs out, the right answer is shown. The aim is to recall the meanings without hints.

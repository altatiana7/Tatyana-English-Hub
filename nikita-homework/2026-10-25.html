Vocabulary · 12 cards

Modal perfects · reasoning about the past

Structure: modal + have + past participle (V3). Use these forms to discuss evidence, uncertainty and alternative interpretations of past events.

must have + V3

Strong inference based on evidence, not a proven fact.
His followers must have found his message convincing.

might / may / could have + V3

Possible explanation; the evidence is inconclusive.
He might have believed his own claims.

can't / couldn't have + V3

Strong inference that something was impossible or very unlikely.
He can't have predicted every consequence.

should have + V3

Criticism or regret about a past action.
The leaders should have addressed the criticism openly.

could have + V3

Also describes an unrealised past option (context decides).
They could have handled the dispute differently.

Careful historical claims

It appears that… / The evidence suggests… / Arguably… / We cannot establish whether…
Do not present guesses about motives as established facts.

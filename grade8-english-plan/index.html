Reading: Train Smarter

At thirteen, Mia had already spent years on cross-country skis. She liked racing, but what she enjoyed most was the feeling of getting better at something difficult. Her training week was busy: three endurance sessions, two technique sessions and one easy recovery day. At first, Mia tried to do everything perfectly, including schoolwork. That made her tired and stressed.

Her coach suggested a simpler system. Mia now plans only three priorities for each day: one for school, one for sport and one for rest. She also keeps a short training diary in English because she wants to compete abroad one day. At first she wrote only basic sentences, but after several months she noticed that both her English and her training notes had become clearer.

Mia still has difficult days. Sometimes she loses a race or gets a disappointing mark at school. Instead of giving up, she asks, “What can I change next time?”

Train smarter, not only harder

At thirteen, Mia had already spent years on skis. She liked competing, but what she enjoyed most was getting better at something difficult. Her training week was busy. At first, Mia tried to do everything perfectly, including schoolwork. That made her tired and stressed.

Her coach suggested a simpler system. Mia now plans only three priorities for each day: one for school, one for sport and one for rest. She also keeps a short training diary in English because she wants to compete abroad one day.

Mia still has difficult days. Instead of giving up, she asks: “What can I change next time?”

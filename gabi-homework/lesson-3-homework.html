DEFINITE ARTICLE

When do we use the?

1. A specific thingWe both know which one: the festival I had been dreaming about.
2. Unique in this situationthe main stage, the headliner, the crowd.
3. Mentioned / identified clearlyA phrase after the noun can make it specific: the highlight of the event.
4. Fixed time phrasesin the morning, throughout the day, by the end of the night.
No the when you mean something in general: live music, bands, pizza.

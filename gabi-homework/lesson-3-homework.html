QUIZLET · WORKSHEET

How to describe a strong smell

These words help you describe what a smell is like, where it comes from, how strong it is, and how it affects you.

1 · The source / typeUse words like rotten, musty, smoky, chemical, garlicky, fishy.
2 · The strengthUse pungent, overpowering, intense, acrid, overwhelming.
3 · The effect on youSay it burns my nose, makes me feel sick, gives me a headache.
Example: The room had a musty, overpowering smell. It made me feel sick.

DEFINITE ARTICLE

When do we use the?

1. A specific thingWe both know which one: the festival I had been dreaming about.
2. Unique in this situationthe main stage, the headliner, the crowd.
3. Mentioned / identified clearlyA phrase after the noun can make it specific: the highlight of the event.
4. Fixed time phrasesin the morning, throughout the day, by the end of the night.
No the when you mean something in general: live music, bands, pizza.

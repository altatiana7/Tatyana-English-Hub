PAST SIMPLE · finished action, the main eventverb-ed / irregular: She met a Frenchman.
did / didn’t + verb: He didn’t have a pass.
One action after another: We arrived, paid and left.
PAST CONTINUOUS · action in progress, the backgroundwas / were + verb-ing: She was living in London.
I / he / she / it was · you / we / they were
At a moment in the past: At 8.00 I was driving home.
TOGETHER · when / whileWhile the crowd was waiting, he took a picture.
We were walking when an owl escaped.
know, like, want, understand: I knew him (was knowing).

LISTENING · 1.19–1.22

Elspeth’s dating experiment

Track 1.19 · The experiment
My name’s Elspeth. I’m twenty-five, I’m a journalist, I live in Dublin, and I’m single. It’s not easy to meet people here. My mother is going to use my dating app, choose the men she likes, and I’ve promised to go on a date with the men she chooses.
Track 1.20 · John & Sebastian
John: He’s very tall, a teacher, and the date is fun, but there isn’t a spark.

Sebastian: He’s tall, dark and handsome, from Germany but living in Dublin. He’s a real gentleman and asks for a second date, but Elspeth doesn’t think it will work.
Track 1.21 · George
Date number three is George. The first bar is closed, so they go somewhere else. George works in IT, is from just outside Dublin, is relaxed and interesting, has travelled a lot, and tells funny stories. The date is going well when Elspeth’s phone pings.
Track 1.22 · The message
The message is from Elspeth’s mother: “Well, is he lovely? Love Mum.” George sees it on the screen. Elspeth feels extremely embarrassed.
Homework Choose John or Sebastian and describe him in English. Write 6–8 sentences about appearance, job/background, personality, the date, and whether he is a good match for Elspeth.

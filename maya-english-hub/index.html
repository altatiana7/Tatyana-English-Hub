Elspeth’s dating experiment

Audio loads only when you open this lesson, so the main page stays fast.

Track 1.19 · The experiment
My name’s Elspeth. I’m twenty-five, I’m a journalist, I live in Dublin, and I’m single. It’s not easy to meet people here. So who can help me? My mother…and a dating app. My favourite dating app, called Tinder, shows you photos of possible partners, with a bit of information about them. You look at the photos and swipe, swipe, swipe. Swipe right if you like them, and swipe left if you don’t. If a guy likes you and you like him, you have a match. Then you can start messaging the person.
Track 1.20 · John & Sebastian
Date 1
My first date is with a nice guy called John. He’s very tall. He’s a teacher. We get on well and it’s a fun date, but sadly, there isn’t a spark.

Date 2
Sebastian is tall, dark, and handsome. He’s from Germany, but he lives in Dublin. He’s a real gentleman and asks for a second date.
Track 1.21 · George
Date number three is George. He works in IT and is from just outside Dublin. He’s very relaxed and interesting, and he’s travelled a lot. He tells a lot of funny stories.
Track 1.22 · The message
It’s all going well when Elspeth’s phone pings. It’s a text from her mother: “Well, is he lovely? Love Mum.” George looks at the screen and reads the text.
Homework Choose John or Sebastian and describe him in English. Write 6–8 sentences.

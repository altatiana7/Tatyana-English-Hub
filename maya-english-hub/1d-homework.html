Describe the picture

Write 8–10 sentences. Do not list objects — make one connected description.

Use this order:
1. Say what the picture shows.
2. Say where the people are.
3. Describe the woman and the man.
4. Say what each person is doing.
5. Mention clothes, objects and the room.
6. Say how the people may feel.
7. Use at least 3 words from 1A–1C.

Useful: in the foreground · in the background · next to · in front of · on the left/right · seems · looks · is wearing · is sitting · is standing

Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Present Simple

routinesI usually meet my friends after work.
factsThe Earth moves around the Sun.
likes / dislikesShe likes simple clothes.
permanent situationsHe lives in Berlin.
stative verbsI know, like, love, want, need, believe, understand, prefer...

Present Continuous

nowShe is reading a letter now.
temporaryHe is staying with friends this week.
changing situationsHer English is getting better.
arrangementsWe are meeting at seven tonight.
annoying repetitionYou are always interrupting me!
Important: do not use stative verbs in Continuous in their normal state meaning: I know, not I am knowing; she wants, not she is wanting.

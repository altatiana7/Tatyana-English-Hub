Задание 38.1 · Humanities

Imagine that you are doing a project on why many young Zetlanders choose to major in the humanities. You have found some data — the results of a survey conducted among young Zetlanders.

Why have you chosen to major in the humanities?%
Love for creativity37
Interesting subjects28
Public significance20
Variety of jobs upon graduation9
Personal fulfilment6

Write 200–250 words. Make an opening statement; report 2–3 facts; make 1–2 relevant comparisons and comment on them; outline a problem connected with studying the humanities and suggest a solution; conclude with your opinion on the importance of choosing the right profession.

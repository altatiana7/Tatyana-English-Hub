HeadlineFull sentenceRule
AI exam cheat confessesAn AI exam cheat has confessed.Present Simple = something that has just happened
Korea to inspect glassesKorea is going to inspect glasses.to + verb = the future
Some calling for…Some people are calling for…-ing = Present Continuous without “be”
Glasses bannedGlasses have been banned.Past Participle = passive without “be”
AI exam cheata person who cheated in an exam using AInoun + noun + noun, no articles
  • Articles (a, an, the) and the verb be usually disappear.
  • Present Simple in a headline is usually about the past: confesses = has confessed.
  • to + verb is about the future, not purpose.
  • A past participle alone is usually passive: banned = has been banned.
  • Short strong words: sparks = causes, outrage = great anger.
